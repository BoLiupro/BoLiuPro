Here is **Bo Liu (刘博)**.

I am a third-year M.S. student in the **College of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering at Hunan University**, and I am also affiliated with the **National Supercomputing Center in Changsha**. I am fortunate to conduct my research under the guidance of **Prof. Zhu Xiao** and **Prof. Tong Li**.

My research journey started from **graph learning and diffusion modeling for spatiotemporal mobility prediction**, where I studied how graph structures, multi-granularity temporal patterns, and generative models can be used to model human and urban dynamics. I then moved toward **LLM-based semantic reasoning for spatiotemporal human behavior**, exploring how large language models can interpret high-level mobility intentions, contextual information, and long-term behavioral patterns beyond purely numerical modeling.

Currently, my primary interest is **Agentic AI over complex structured environments**, especially for **Spatiotemporal Intelligence** and **Mobile Data Mining**. I am interested in building LLM-based agents that can perceive structured environments, maintain and retrieve knowledge, collaborate with other agents, reason over graphs and temporal dynamics, and iteratively improve their decisions through reflection. Looking forward, I am also broadly interested in **Agentic AI, Human–Computer Interaction, and Embodied Intelligence**.

My work has been published or accepted by venues including **IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems (T-ITS)**, **IEEE Transactions on Services Computing (TSC)**, and the **Chinese Journal of Computers**. I am always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations.
