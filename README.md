I am a third-year Master’s student at the **College of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering, Hunan University**, and the **National Supercomputing Center in Changsha**. I am fortunate to be advised by **[Prof. Zhu Xiao](https://csee.hnu.edu.cn/people/xiaozhu)** and **[Prof. Tong Li](https://tong89.github.io/tongli.github.io/)**. My work has been published or accepted in **IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems (T-ITS)**, **IEEE Transactions on Services Computing (TSC)**, and the **Chinese Journal of Computers**, with ongoing work under review or revision at major AI and data-mining venues.

My research has developed from **graph learning and diffusion modeling** for spatiotemporal systems, to **LLM-based semantic reasoning** for human mobility and mobile-app behavior, and further toward **retrieval-augmented, personalized, and multi-agent systems** that reason over complex structured environments. More recently, I have been interested in how agentic systems can move beyond purely symbolic or tabular structures and develop stronger capabilities for **spatial understanding, multimodal perception, and grounded reasoning**.

My current research interests are:
- **Agentic AI** — LLM-based agents that reason, retrieve, coordinate, use tools, and act over complex structured environments.
- **Spatial Intelligence** — understanding and reasoning about spatial, spatiotemporal, and physical-world structure.
- **Multimodal Foundation Models** — especially vision-language models and multimodal representations that connect perception with high-level reasoning.

My previous work in **Spatiotemporal Intelligence and Mobile Data Mining** provides the main foundation for these directions. I am particularly interested in connecting structured reasoning with multimodal perception, so that intelligent agents can better understand and interact with complex real-world environments.
