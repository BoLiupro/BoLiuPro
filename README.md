I am a third-year Master’s student at the **College of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering, Hunan University**, and the **National Supercomputing Center in Changsha**. I am fortunate to be advised by **[Prof. Zhu Xiao](https://csee.hnu.edu.cn/people/xiaozhu)** and **[Prof. Tong Li](https://tong89.github.io/tongli.github.io/)**. My work has been published or accepted in **IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems (T-ITS)**, **IEEE Transactions on Services Computing (TSC)**, and the **Chinese Journal of Computers**, with ongoing work under review or revision at major AI and data-mining venues.

My research has developed through four connected stages, moving from spatiotemporal modeling toward agents that reason about people and structured environments. I began with **graph learning and diffusion modeling**, studying structured representations of human mobility and multi-granularity graph diffusion for private-car activity prediction. I then moved to **LLM-based semantic reasoning**, exploring how large language models can infer travel intent, interpret behavioral context, and guide mobility generation and hierarchical mobile-app usage prediction. Building on this, I investigated **retrieval-augmented and personalized agents**, combining collective behavioral knowledge with individual memory through retrieval and adaptive reflection. More recently, I have been studying **multi-agent reasoning over evolving structured environments**, where agents reason jointly over users, locations, graphs, temporal dynamics, and community-level interaction patterns.

Looking ahead, I am interested in how intelligent agents **perceive, reason, interact, and act in complex human-centered and physical environments**. My current interests broadly include **Agentic AI, Large Language Models, Spatiotemporal and Spatial Intelligence, Human–AI Interaction, Robotics, and Embodied Intelligence**. Alongside these directions, I am also exploring **agentic code intelligence** and the retrieval and reuse of structured software assets.
